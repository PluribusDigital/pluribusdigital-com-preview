Pluribus Digital Welcomes Jon Carr as Our Chief Growth Officer

by Pluribus Digital

Jon Carr has joined Pluribus Digital as the new Chief Growth Officer. In this capacity, Jon will define and implement the growth strategy to expand our federal presence across Design, Development, and Delivery capabilities within target markets of Health and Benefits, Homeland Security, and Federal Financial agencies.

Jon brings over 18 years of experience within the federal market, bringing a deep level of understanding required to position Pluribus Digital for the future. Prior to joining Pluribus Digital, Jon held various growth leadership roles at digital services companies, where he was responsible for net new growth, while building the brand, capabilities, and presence within target markets.

Jon holds a bachelor’s degree from James Madison University and is still actively involved in the alumni network as the Co-Chair for Alumni Engagement for the JMU Federal Dukes.

We are excited to have Jon onboard and look forward to the dedication, energy, and creativity he brings to the company.

Pluribus Digital helps government change agents realize their digital services vision by providing the implementation capabilities to design, develop, and deliver high-impact digital products that serve the public good.
